Macrowise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Macrowise is a food and macro log for iPhone, made by Roy Kelly ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: we don't collect any of your data. Your food log stays on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud.

What we collect

Nothing. Macrowise has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run a server, and we never receive your food log, your photos or your health data.

Your food log

The foods, meals, targets and settings you enter are stored on your iPhone, inside the app. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also kept in your own private iCloud account so they sync between your devices and come back if you reinstall. We can't see your iCloud data. It's handled by Apple under your iCloud settings, and you can turn it off for Macrowise in Settings, then your name, then iCloud. They're also included in your normal iPhone backups.

Apple Health

Macrowise only uses Apple Health data you allow in the Health permission screen. You can change this at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings.

  • What it writes: the calories, protein, carbohydrates, total fat, fibre, sugars, saturated fat and sodium you log.
  • Nutrition it reads: its own entries, so it can keep Health in step when you edit or delete them, and food that other apps save to Health, which Macrowise adds to your log automatically, including in the background. Macrowise never writes imported food back to Health.
  • Activity and body data it reads for the Progress tab: calories burned (active and resting), steps, walking and running distance, exercise minutes, flights climbed, stand hours, workouts, weight, body fat and lean body mass, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, VO2 max, sleep, water and your Activity summary (the Move goal from your Apple Watch). This is shown to you next to your food so you can see how your eating and activity line up. Macrowise also uses your recent activity and sleep to estimate, on your iPhone, how many calories and steps you'll reach by bedtime.

Health data is used only to provide this feature. It is never sent to us, never shared with anyone else, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold.

Turning AI apps off

You can switch off Use an AI app in Settings. Macrowise then hides every feature that shares with an AI app, and the Progress summary is worked out on your iPhone instead.

Progress reviews with an AI app

On the Progress tab you can ask an AI app to review a day, week or month. When you tap Send, Macrowise puts together a text summary of your food and the Health figures above for that period and opens the iPhone share sheet (or copies it, if you choose). You pick which app receives it, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Nothing is sent until you do, and Macrowise never sends Health data to us. What that app does with the summary is covered by that app's own privacy policy. Reviews you save are kept in the app and your private iCloud like the rest of your log.

Camera and photos

Macrowise uses the camera and your photo library only when you choose to take or pick a photo of a meal, a food label or a barcode. Photos are not uploaded anywhere by Macrowise. When you use Read a label, the text is read on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition, and the photo never leaves your phone.

Reading photos and descriptions with an AI app

When you tap Send, Macrowise opens the iPhone share sheet with your photos (or your typed description) and a short set of instructions. You choose which app receives them, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Nothing is sent until you pick an app. What that app does with your photos and text is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

After you copy the reply and come back, Macrowise reads the clipboard once to pick up the reply. It only uses the clipboard if it contains a Macrowise reply, and only while you're waiting for one.

If you build a shortcut with the Macrowise actions and Apple's Use Model action, your photo goes to the model you choose in that shortcut, under Apple's terms (or OpenAI's, if you pick ChatGPT). Macrowise only provides the instructions and receives the reply.

Barcode lookups and food search

Everyday foods (from the UK, US and Canadian government food tables) are built into the app, so searching them never leaves your iPhone. Searches for branded and supermarket foods send the words you type to Open Food Facts. When you scan a barcode, Macrowise sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up the product. No personal information is sent with either. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to those requests. Product details are then saved on your iPhone so repeat scans are quicker. If a food you logged earlier is missing fibre, sugars, saturated fat or salt, Macrowise may look its barcode up again in the same way to fill them in.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry or saved meal in the app. Deleting an entry also removes it from Apple Health. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Entries already in Apple Health can also be managed in the Health app.

Children

Macrowise is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
