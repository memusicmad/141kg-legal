Macrowise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Macrowise is a food and macro log for iPhone, made by Roy Kelly ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: we don't collect any of your data. Your food log stays on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. Macrowise has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run a server, and we never receive your food log, your photos or your health data.

Your food log

The foods, meals, targets and settings you enter are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. They're included in your normal iPhone backups, which are handled by Apple under your own settings.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Macrowise writes the dietary energy (calories), protein, carbohydrates and total fat you log to Apple Health, and reads those same entries back so it can keep Health in step when you edit or delete them. You choose this in the Health permission screen and can change it at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings.

Health data is used only to provide this feature. It is never sent to us, never shared with anyone else, never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold.

Camera and photos

Macrowise uses the camera and your photo library only when you choose to take or pick a photo of a meal, a food label or a barcode. Photos are not uploaded anywhere by Macrowise.

Reading photos and descriptions with an AI app

When you tap Send, Macrowise opens the iPhone share sheet with your photos (or your typed description) and a short set of instructions. You choose which app receives them, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Nothing is sent until you pick an app. What that app does with your photos and text is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

After you copy the reply and come back, Macrowise reads the clipboard once to pick up the reply. It only uses the clipboard if it contains a Macrowise reply, and only while you're waiting for one.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, Macrowise sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up the product. No personal information is sent with it. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to that request. Product details are then saved on your iPhone so repeat scans are quicker.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry or saved meal in the app. Deleting an entry also removes it from Apple Health. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Entries already in Apple Health can also be managed in the Health app.

Children

Macrowise is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
