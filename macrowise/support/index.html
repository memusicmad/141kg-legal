Common questions

How do photos work? Tap Photo, take up to 3 photos, choose how much you'll have, then tap Send. Pick your AI app (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) in the share list, answer any questions, tap Copy under the final reply and go back to Macrowise. The full guide is in the app under Settings > How Macrowise works.

The reply wasn't picked up. Tap Paste on the Photo screen. To stop iPhone asking each time, go to iPhone Settings > Apps > Macrowise > Paste from Other Apps > Allow.

I don't have an AI app. Barcode scanning and manual entry work without one. You can download Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini free from the App Store.

Nothing appears in Apple Health. Open Macrowise Settings, turn on Write to Apple Health and tap Allow Apple Health access.
